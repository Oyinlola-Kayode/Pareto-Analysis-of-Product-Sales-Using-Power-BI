# 📊 Pareto Analysis of Product Sales

---

## 🎯 Objective  
To analyze product sales distribution using the Pareto principle, aiming to identify the key products driving the majority of sales and assess how closely the sales distribution aligns with the classic 80/20 rule.

---

## 🧪 Methodology  
The datasets were integrated through data modeling in Power BI, establishing relationships between relevant tables. DAX measures were created to compute key metrics such as Total Sales, Sales Rank, Cumulative Sales, Cumulative Percentage, Sales Percentage, and custom parameters.  
The insights were visualized using interactive Power BI visuals to support the analysis.

---

## 📂 Data Overview  
- Total Products Analyzed: 35  
- Total Sales: $14.4M  
- Top-Contributing Products: 15 products (approximately 43% of the total)  
- Sales Contribution of Top Products: 80% of total sales  

This indicates that 43% of the products are responsible for 80% of the sales, deviating from the traditional Pareto principle, which suggests that 20% of causes contribute to 80% of the effects.

---

## 🔍 Key Findings  
- Sales Concentration: The top 15 products are the primary revenue drivers, collectively contributing to 80% of the sales.  
- Remaining Products: The remaining 20 products, while individually contributing less, collectively account for the remaining 20% of sales.  

This distribution suggests a more balanced sales spread across products than the classic Pareto distribution.

## 💡 Insights  
- Broader Product Importance: A larger portion of products contributes significantly to sales, indicating that the business does not rely solely on a few top-performing products.  
- Risk Mitigation: Diversified sales reduce dependency on a limited number of products, potentially mitigating risks associated with market fluctuations affecting specific products.

---

## ✅ Recommendations  

### 🔝 Strategic Focus on Top Products  
- Inventory Management: Ensure optimal stock levels for the top 15 products which includes Toys, Games, Electronics, Arts & Crafts, Sports & Outdoor, to prevent stockouts and meet customer demand.  
- Marketing Efforts: Allocate more marketing resources to promote these high-performing products further.

### 🧾 Evaluation of Remaining Products  
- Performance Analysis: Assess the profitability and sales trends of the remaining 20 products.  
- Improvement Strategies: Identify opportunities to boost sales, such as bundling, promotions, or product enhancements.

### ⏲ Regular Monitoring  
- Periodic Analysis: Conduct regular Pareto analyses to track changes in sales distribution and adjust strategies accordingly.

---

## 🧠 Conclusion  
The analysis reveals a more evenly distributed sales pattern than the classic 80/20 rule suggests. This balanced distribution implies that while certain products are top performers, a significant portion of the product line contributes meaningfully to overall sales.  
Strategic focus should be maintained on high-performing products, while also exploring growth opportunities among the broader product range.
